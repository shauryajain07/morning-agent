Morning Agent is an organizational aid. It is not a substitute for checking the original source or making your own decisions.

AI-assisted decisions can be wrong

Classification and prioritization may miss context, misunderstand a message, or misstate urgency. Check important messages, deadlines, files, and course requirements directly with their source. Do not rely on the app alone for critical or time-sensitive decisions.

Review actions before approving them

Suggestions are not instructions. Confirm the destination, content, and timing of any task, calendar event, file download, or file move before approving or running it. Keep backups of important files and data.

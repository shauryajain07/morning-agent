Private by design

Your review stays on your Mac.

Morning Agent stores collected items and review history in a local SQLite database. It has no project account or built-in analytics. Local Laya-MLX is available; if you configure TypeSafe Jev, the item text needed for classification is sent to that provider.

Before you download

Good to know.

Is Morning Agent a hosted service?

No. The app runs on your computer. The landing page doesn’t connect to your accounts or show your dashboard.

Is the download a Mac installer?

Not yet. The download is source code. You’ll need Node.js 20 or later and a few Terminal commands; the app is macOS-first.

Do I have to connect an account?

No. Every source is optional. You can first run the offline sample workflow without connecting any accounts.

Can I use it commercially?

The current license permits noncommercial use. See the Terms and license notes for details.

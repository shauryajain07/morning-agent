Built to stay close to you

Your morning is
your business.

Morning Agent is a local app, not a hosted inbox. It keeps its history in a SQLite database on your computer. Browser sign-ins and tokens remain in local files you control.

Before you download

A few useful details.

Is this a hosted service?

No. Morning Agent runs on your computer. The website is only a product page; it does not connect to your accounts or show your dashboard.

Is this a one-click Mac app?

Not yet. The download is the project source code. You will need Node.js 20 or newer and a little Terminal setup. The app is macOS-first.

Do I need to connect every source?

No. Sources are optional. You configure the services you want to use, and some sources need their own account sign-in or API credentials.

Can I use it at work or for a business?

The current license permits noncommercial use only. Commercial use requires separate permission. See the Terms and license notes.
